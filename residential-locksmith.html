Residential locksmith in Tampa: rekeys, lock changes and deadbolts

Priority Lock and Security is a commercial locksmith first, and we rekey and change locks for homeowners in the Tampa area too. New house, lost keys, a roommate or a contractor who still has a key: we rekey every exterior door to one new key on site, replace worn locks and deadbolts, and cut the copies you need.

Just moved in? Rekey.

You cannot know how many keys to your new house are out there: the previous owners, their family, the cleaner, the dog walker, the contractor who finished the kitchen. A rekey changes the pins inside your existing locks so the old keys stop working and the locks stay on the door. Every exterior door can be put on one key, and it is done in one visit, with the copies cut on the spot.

Lost keys, a breakup, a roommate moving out

Same answer: rekey the doors that key opened. Getting a key back does not tell you whether it was copied. If the hardware is in good shape, rekeying is cheaper than new locks and the door looks exactly the same afterward.

Lock changes and deadbolts

When a knob is loose, a deadbolt stops short, or a lock is simply worn out, we replace it with a quality lock keyed to the rest of the house. A deadbolt is only locked when the bolt is thrown all the way out; if your key stops short or grinds, or you have to pull the door toward you to turn it, the strike or the frame needs correcting, and we fix that at the same time. We also add deadbolts to doors that never had one.

Landlords and rentals

If you rent out a house or a few units, we rekey at every turnover so the new tenant holds the only keys, and we can put your rentals on a small master key system so one key of yours opens all of them while each tenant's key opens only their own door. See tenant turnover and master key systems.

What we do not do at homes

Car keys, car lockouts and safes. We will point you to somebody good who does those. Questions about home lock work

How much does it cost to rekey a house?

Residential lock work is $100 an hour, the first hour is the visit, and key copies are $3 each. Most houses are done in one visit. After hours, evenings and weekends by arrangement, is $149 for the first hour. Text a photo of your doors to 813-421-4787 and you get a price before anyone is scheduled.

Should I rekey or replace the locks on a new house?

Rekey, if the locks are in good condition: the old keys stop working and the hardware stays. Replace a lock only when it is worn, loose or the wrong kind for the door. Many houses need a rekey on most doors and a new deadbolt on one.

Can all my doors work on one key?

Usually, yes. Locks of the same brand or keyway are pinned alike so one key opens the front door, the back door and the garage entry. Where the locks are a mix of brands, one or two may need a new cylinder or a new lock.

Do you do house lockouts?

We are a scheduled service company rather than an emergency lockout line, so during business hours text 813-421-4787 and we will tell you plainly what we can do and when. We do not do car lockouts.
