Who does commercial locksmith work in Tampa?

Priority Lock and Security does, and it is the main thing we do. We are a locksmith company in Tampa, Florida, founded in June 2021 and owned by Steve Wathen, and most of our work is on commercial and multi-tenant buildings in and around Ybor City, downtown Tampa and the neighborhoods within about thirty minutes of downtown. Property managers, building owners, office managers and facilities teams call us for the doors that stopped working right and for the keys that nobody has under control.

We are insured. Florida does not license locksmiths at the state level, and Hillsborough County stopped issuing and monitoring its locksmith licenses on July 1, 2025, so we do not advertise a license number. What a building owner can ask us for is a certificate of insurance and references from buildings like theirs.

What we work on

  • Commercial lever locks, mortise locks and deadbolts: repair, rekey and replacement, grade 1 and grade 2 hardware, ADA lever hardware on suite entries, office doors and interior passages.
  • Panic bars and exit devices: rim, mortise and vertical rod devices on storefronts and fire exits, adjusted or replaced so the door latches every time and opens with one push.
  • Door closers: doors that slam, will not close, will not latch or are hard to open. Adjustment, repair and replacement, sized to the door.
  • Aluminum storefront door hardware: hook bolts and deadlatches, cylinders, pivots, closers and alignment on glass storefront doors.
  • Rekeys and lock changes: staff changes, lost keys, new ownership, and mailbox locks in multi-tenant buildings. Keys cut for your building's key system.
  • Tenant turnover: suites, apartments and offices rekeyed inside your existing key system when a tenant leaves.
  • Master key systems and key control: keying plans for multi-tenant buildings and office complexes, with the records kept.
  • Door hardware repair: hinges, strikes, latches and alignment on hollow metal and aluminum doors that stopped shutting right, and temporary lockup hardware to secure an opening until the permanent fix.
  • Ongoing building support: one number for managers and facilities teams handling doors across several properties. Bids and scheduled work welcome.

Who we work for

Property managers and building owners are the core of the work: older multi-tenant buildings in Ybor City, office buildings downtown, storefronts, restaurants, churches, schools and apartment communities. What a manager needs from a locksmith is mostly not about locks. It is somebody who answers, shows up when they said they would, tells you what they found in plain words, and keeps a record of your doors and keys so the next request does not start from zero. That is how we run every account.

We also rekey and change locks for homeowners. See residential locksmith. We do not do car keys, car lockouts or safes, and we will point you to somebody good who does.

Questions we get

Questions about commercial locksmith work in Tampa

What is a commercial locksmith?

A commercial locksmith works on the door hardware of businesses and multi-tenant buildings rather than houses and cars: commercial grade lever and mortise locks, panic bars and exit devices, door closers, aluminum storefront door hardware, rekeying, tenant turnover lock changes and master key systems. The hardware is heavier, it is rated by ANSI/BHMA grade, it has to meet code on exit doors, and the key systems are planned so one manager can control access across many doors. Priority Lock and Security does this work across Tampa and Ybor City.

Do you serve Ybor City?

Yes. Most of our work is in and around Ybor City, plus downtown Tampa, the Channel District, Tampa Heights, Seminole Heights, Hyde Park, South Tampa, West Tampa, Westshore, Temple Terrace, Town 'N' Country, Carrollwood, Citrus Park, Brandon, Riverview and Lutz. If your building is a little outside that and you manage other properties inside it, ask.

Are you licensed and insured?

Insured, yes: Priority Lock and Security carries general liability insurance and can provide a certificate of insurance to a property manager or owner. Licensed, no, and neither is anyone else: Florida does not license locksmiths at the state level, and Hillsborough County stopped issuing, monitoring and registering its locksmith licenses on July 1, 2025, after the state preempted local occupational licensing. Any company still advertising a county locksmith license number is citing a program that no longer runs.

Do you work on houses too?

Yes. Commercial door hardware is the main work, and we also rekey and change locks for homeowners in the Tampa area: rekeys, lock changes and deadbolts. We do not do car keys, car lockouts or safes.

We are a scheduled service company rather than a 24 hour emergency line. For weekends and after hours, ask and we will see what we can work out.
