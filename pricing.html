Typical jobs

  • Rekey a home with up to about 4 locks: usually fits in the first hour, $125.
  • Rekey a larger home, 5 to 8 locks: about 1 to 1.5 hours, $125 to $175, plus any extra keys.
  • Adjust a door or closer that won't latch: usually inside the first hour.
  • Replace a door closer: about 1 to 2 hours of labor, plus the closer.
  • Install a panic bar: about 2 to 3 hours of labor, plus the device.

Time is billed in quarter hours after the first hour. These are typical; your quote is the real number.

What changes the price

  • How many locks or doors, and whether they're all the same brand.
  • Parts: new locks, closers or exit devices are priced on your quote before work starts.
  • Older or worn hardware that needs more time to get right.

How quoting works

  • Text us how many locks, or how many doors are giving you trouble. We text back a ballpark within minutes during business hours.
  • A standard rekey: the ballpark is your price.
  • Bigger than that, or over $500: a free estimate visit and a firm quote before any work.
  • Deposits: only when parts are over $500, paid before we order them. Labor is billed when the work is done.
  • Realtor referral: buyers of registered realtors get $25 off their visit. Realtors register here.
