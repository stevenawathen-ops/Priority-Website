Ybor City first

Most of our work is in and around Ybor City: older multi-tenant buildings, offices, restaurants and storefronts along and off 7th Avenue, for the property managers and owners who keep them running. A locksmith who already knows the building saves you the walk-through every time, which is why we keep records of the doors, hardware and keys of every building we service.

Just outside the list

If your property is a little outside these areas and you manage other properties inside them, ask. We would rather look after all of one customer's doors than draw a hard line on a map. Text a photo of the door to 813-421-4787 with the address and we will tell you plainly whether we can get there and when.
