What we do, how long it takes, what it costs

Priority Lock and Security is a scheduled locksmith and door hardware company in Tampa, Florida, working mostly in and around Ybor City. Homes are $125 the first hour, trip included, then $100 an hour. Businesses are $125 an hour. Parts are priced on your quote before any work starts. Estimates are free. Updated November 2026.

Rekey a house

We rekey your locks so every old key stops working, and match them to one key where the locks allow it. Most rekeys of up to about 4 locks fit in the first hour.

About 10 to 15 minutes a lock$125 the first hour, then $100 an hourUp to 4 copies of each key on a new home

For: new homeowners, someone moved out who kept a key, after a break in, renters (we can work with your landlord), landlords between tenants.

Lock and deadbolt replacement

We replace worn or broken knobs, levers and deadbolts, and can key the new ones to match the rest of the house or building.

About 20 to 45 minutes a doorLabor at the hourly rate, plus the lock

For: homes and businesses with a lock that sticks, spins, won't lock, or was damaged.

Door adjustment and hinges

We get doors sitting right in their frames again: hinges tightened or replaced, strikes adjusted, so the bolt throws clean and the door latches every time. We do not replace door frames.

About 30 to 60 minutes a doorUsually inside the first hour

For: doors that stick, sag, rub, or have to be pushed or lifted to lock.

Text us the doors driving you nuts

Door closers

We adjust, repair and replace surface and concealed door closers so doors close at a safe speed and latch on their own.

Adjust: about 15 to 30 minutesReplace: about 1 to 2 hours, plus the closer$125 an hour for businesses

For: doors that slam, drift open, won't latch, or leak oil. We set a safe closing speed and a light opening force and check the latch, so the door is ready for a routine fire or accessibility check. We can't promise any inspection result, but we'll tell you plainly what we'd fix.

Panic bars and exit hardware

For: restaurants, offices, churches, schools, clinics and apartment buildings. An exit door that won't latch needs fixing, not skipping. We check that the door releases and latches properly.
