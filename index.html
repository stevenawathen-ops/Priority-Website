What we do

The hardware a building actually runs on

Offices, retail, churches, schools, multi-tenant. If it opens, latches, or locks and it's on a commercial door, it's in scope.

01

Commercial hardware

Install, repair, and replace grade-2 lever sets on suite entries, office doors, and interior passages — the hardware you touch every day.

02

Panic hardware & exit devices

Push bars, crash bars, and rim exit devices on storefronts and fire exits — installed and adjusted to latch the way code expects.

03

Rekeys & lock changes

Tenant turnover, staff changes, lost keys. Rekeyed on-site with fast turnaround so the building never sits unsecured.

04

Door hardware repair

Closers, hinges, strikes, latches, and alignment on hollow metal and aluminum storefront doors that stopped shutting right.

05

Master key systems

Keying schedules for multi-tenant buildings and office complexes — organized access without the cost of going electronic.

06

Ongoing building support

One number for property managers and facilities teams handling doors across several properties. Bids and scheduled work welcome.
