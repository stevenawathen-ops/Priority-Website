Why is my door slamming shut?

The closer's speed valves have drifted, the closer is leaking fluid, or the closer is the wrong size for the door. Tampa heat thins closer fluid on sun-facing doors. Most slamming doors can be adjusted. A closer that is leaking oil has to be replaced, because once the seals are gone it cannot be refilled.

My panic bar won't latch on a fire exit. Is that urgent?

Yes. An exit device that does not latch leaves the opening unsecured, and on a fire-rated door it is a life safety issue, not a convenience issue. Send a photo of the device and the strike and we will tell you what it takes.

Should I rekey when a tenant moves out or when I take over a building?

Yes, and it is the standard recommendation. You cannot know how many copies of the old keys exist, and a rekey makes every one of them useless for far less than replacing hardware. In a master keyed building the rekey should go through the locksmith who keeps the building's key records, so the suite stays on the master.

Does a locksmith in Hillsborough County need a license?

Florida does not license locksmiths at the state level, and Hillsborough County stopped issuing and monitoring its locksmith licenses on July 1, 2025, after the state preempted local occupational licensing. Any company still advertising a county locksmith license number is citing a program that no longer runs. What is worth asking a vendor for instead is a certificate of insurance and references from buildings like yours. Priority Lock and Security is insured.
