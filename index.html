Residential  ·  Starts at $100/hr, one hour minimum

Just moved in? Change the locks before you unpack.

You do not know who has a key to your new house. The builder, the last owner, a contractor, a dog walker, a relative, a roommate from four years ago. A rekey ends all of it in about an hour, and it is the first thing worth doing for the security of the place you sleep.

Service 1

Rekey

We re-pin the locks you already own so every old key stops working and one new key runs the house. No hardware to buy, nothing to match, no holes in the door. This is the first move for a new homeowner and it is what the $100 starting price is built for.

Service 2

Lock and hardware installation

New deadbolts, entry sets, handlesets and door hardware, installed correctly and aligned so the door actually latches. Upgrading a hollow builder-grade lock on a front door is the most visible security improvement most houses can make.

Service 3

Smart lock setup

Keypad and connected locks installed and set up, so you can let the dog sitter in from your phone and stop hiding a key under a rock. Two honest rules: ongoing programming stays with you, because manufacturers change their apps whenever they like, and parts bought through us are the only ones we can warranty.

Realtors and new movers

The $100 starting price is a realtor referral rate. It exists so an agent can hand a new buyer a locksmith with the price already settled, no bidding, no surprise on closing week. If you sell houses in Tampa and you want something useful to put in the folder, text 813-421-4787 and we will sort it out.

The residential lane is these three services. Anything beyond them, including anything at a rental you own or manage, is quoted as commercial. An individual homeowner is residential. Everything else, including every landlord and every tenant of one, is commercial. The customer decides the lane, never the property type.

Commercial  ·  Starts at $125/hr, one hour minimum

The doors your business uses a hundred times a day

Commercial door hardware fails in specific, boring, expensive ways. A lever that will not retract. A panic bar that will not latch on a fire exit. A closer that slams. A strike that drifted a sixteenth of an inch and now nothing lines up. We work on that hardware every day and we fix the door, not just the lock.

Commercial lever locks

Installation, repair and replacement of commercial grade lever locks on office doors, suite entries and interior passages. The hardware every business touches daily, matched to the right grade and function instead of whatever the box store had.

Panic and exit hardware

Push bars, crash bars and rim exit devices on storefront doors, fire exits and commercial entries. Installed and repaired so the door latches, dogs down when it should, and does what code expects of an exit.

Rekeying and lock changes

Tenant turnover, staff departure, lost key, contractor who never gave the key back. Rekeyed fast so the building is secure the same day, or changed out completely when the hardware itself is finished.

Door hardware repair

Closers, hinges, strikes, latch mechanisms and alignment on hollow metal and aluminum storefront doors. Most doors that "need a new lock" need the door fixed. We tell you which one it is.

Master key systems

Designed and implemented for multi tenant buildings, office complexes and commercial properties. Organized access without electronic overhead, with the key chart kept on file under key control so the next change starts from a known state.

Key control and documentation

Who holds which key, who authorized it, and when it changed hands, written down. It is the unglamorous part, and it is the part that protects you when someone asks how a door got opened.

Typical customers: property managers, facilities teams, office managers, landlords, retail storefronts, churches, schools and multi tenant residential buildings across Tampa.

Electronic security  ·  Quoted per door, per camera, per drop

Card readers, cameras, intercom. The door opens and nobody thinks about it.

Locks are the floor, not the ceiling. When a building outgrows keys, the answer is a credential you can turn off from a laptop, a camera that recorded it, and an intercom that lets a resident buzz in a delivery without walking down four flights. We install and service all three, plus the network they quietly depend on.

Access control

Card readers, fobs, mobile credentials, electric strikes and maglocks, controllers and cloud setup, plus the first credential load so the system is usable when we leave. Swapping a reader on existing wiring is a different job from a full retrofit that needs power, wire and a strike, so installs are quoted per door rather than by the hour. That way you can put our number next to an integrator's bid and compare like with like.

Cameras and recording

Camera installation with the cable run included, recorder setup, remote viewing on your phone, and retention configured to how long you actually need footage. Quoted per camera, with a separate line for the ones that need a lift, conduit or an exterior run.

Video intercom

Video door entry for apartment, office and multi tenant buildings. A visitor presses a button, the right person sees who it is, and the door opens without anyone hunting for a key or propping it with a brick.

Network and cabling

Cat6 and Cat6A drops, switches with a real PoE budget, access points and device network configuration. This is the install layer that cameras and access control run on. Every drop gets tested for continuity and link. We do not issue cable certification reports, and we will say so before you ask.

Remote support

Quoted on request, and often the fastest way through a problem. A user who cannot get in, a credential that needs revoking today, a camera that dropped off the network, a recorder that stopped retaining. Often solved without anyone driving anywhere, which is the fastest fix there is.

What we will not sell you

No cybersecurity. No network defense, no monitoring, nothing sold as cyber. This is a physical security company and network work happens only as the layer your security devices need. If you need actual cybersecurity, we will point you at someone who does it rather than quote you for something we are not.

Working in Ybor City? Read this before you set a date.

The Ybor City Historic District is governed by the Barrio Latino Commission, and every exterior alteration needs a Certificate of Appropriateness. Like for like repair in the original material can clear staff review in days to weeks. Anything that changes the street facing facade, which includes a surface mounted card reader, an exterior camera or an intercom on a contributing storefront, goes to a hearing that runs two to three months, and the certificate has to be in hand before you can even apply for a permit. We check which track your job falls on before we give you a timeline. A locksmith driving in from another county will not.

Questions

Straight answers

Do you work on houses or only businesses?

Both. Residential covers rekeys, lock and hardware installation, and smart lock setup for an individual homeowner at the home they live in. Everything else, including every landlord and every business tenant, is commercial. The customer decides the lane, not the property type.

What does it cost to have you come out?

There is no trip charge and there never was. Residential starts at $100 per hour and commercial starts at $125 per hour, both with a one hour minimum. Access control, camera and network work is quoted per door, per camera or per drop rather than hourly. Time beyond the first hour is talked through with you before it is billed, so nothing on the invoice is a surprise.

How do I reach you?

Text 813-421-4787. Text is the fastest way and photos of the hardware help a lot. You can also call 813-421-4787 or email steven@prioritylocks.com. Steven Wathen owns the company and answers directly. No call center, no phone tree.

Do you do car keys or vehicle lockouts?

No. Automotive locksmith work is outside what we do. We work on buildings.

Can you rekey during a tenant turnover?

Yes. Turnover rekeys and lock changes are core commercial work, including on multi tenant properties running a master key system. The key chart stays on file under key control, so the next turnover starts from a known state instead of a guess.

Do you install card readers and cameras, or just locks?

Both. Access control, cameras and video intercom are a full service line, along with the cabling, switches and access points those systems need. Installations are quoted per door, per camera or per drop rather than hourly, so you can compare the number against another integrator's bid.

Do you do cybersecurity or network security?

No. We are a physical security company. Network work happens only as the install layer that cameras and access control need, meaning cabling, switching, access points and device configuration. Network defense and monitoring are outside our scope and we will refer that out rather than quote it.

Is there a warranty on the work?

Workmanship carries a 90 day warranty. Part warranties apply to parts purchased through us. If you supply your own hardware, no part warranty applies and programming is not guaranteed, because we cannot stand behind a component we did not source.

Who owns Priority Lock & Security?

Steven Wathen owns the company outright. It is a single member Florida LLC, registered as PRIORITY LOCK AND SECURITY LLC, operating in Tampa since 2021. The person you text is the person accountable for the work.

How do I pay?
