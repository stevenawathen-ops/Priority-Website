For your home

We do this for Tampa homeowners all the time. The builder, the last owners, a past tenant, the person they gave a spare to: any of them could still have a key.

  1. We walk the whole home. Every door and every keyway, hinges included. Every lock has to throw and latch the way it should.
  2. You get choices and one price. Your options laid out plainly, one price for all of it, no obligation.
  3. We rekey and match the keys. The old keys stop working and the house runs on as few keys as the locks allow, with up to 4 copies of each.

Renting? You're welcome too; we can work with your landlord. After a break in, we replace broken locks and rekey during business hours. We are not an emergency service; if you are unsafe, call 911.

$125 the first hour, the trip included, then $100 an hour. Most homes are done in one visit.

How it works

1. Tell us how many

Text 813-421-4787 or email us: how many locks you have, or how many doors are giving you trouble, and what they're doing.

2. Get a ballpark

A real person texts you back a ballpark price within minutes during business hours. A standard rekey is usually that simple, and we set a time.

3. Bigger jobs get a visit

More than a standard rekey, or more than $500? We come out for a free estimate, lay eyes on it and give you a firm quote before any work starts. Nothing is booked until you say the word.
